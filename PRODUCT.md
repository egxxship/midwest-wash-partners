## Platform

web

## Users
- **Wash owners** thinking about selling an in-bay automatic or self-serve wash, often tired, aging equipment, no membership program.
- **Fuel and convenience-store operators** with an underused wash bay or pad behind the pumps.
- **Owners and developers with a site in mind** who need design/build, equipment, service, soap, management, or project financing, in any combination.
- Secondary: prospective hires and trade partners. No investor audience on the public site.

## Product Purpose
Corporate site for Midwest Wash Partners (MWP): an owner, operator, developer and service provider of car washes in the Midwest. Success = qualified inbound from sellers, station operators, and project owners through one contact form.

## Positioning
Owner-operator first: MWP runs its own washes, so every service it sells (build, equip, service, soap, manage, finance) is one it relies on at its own sites. Focus is in-bay automatic / touchless and self-serve washes, not PE-style express tunnels.

## Operating Context
Service area: Illinois, southern Wisconsin, northwest Indiana, southwest Michigan. All leads route to one inbox (esimons@midwestwashpartners.com) for now. Phone line does not exist yet: `[PHONE]` placeholder.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Build: built-to-suit (turnkey project management), design/build (idea to opening), equipment distribution (scope and deliver the package).
- Run: service and repair, soap calibration and supply, wash management (operations, marketing/memberships, accounting, staffing).
- Fund: project financing partner — worded as arranging/structuring capital, never a loan offer.
- Fuel & C-Store Partners program: MWP leases the wash, funds the rebuild, operates it, pays the station rent; cross-promotion at the pump; fleets.
- Next.js 16 app on Vercel; repo egxxship/midwest-wash-partners.

## Product Principles
1. We operate what we sell — lead with owner-operator credibility.
